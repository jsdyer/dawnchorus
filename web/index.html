Scope & collection

Six focal species, with locality names matching British Columbia or the Atlantic provinces: Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland.

The collector requests the first 100 results for each of 30 species/province combinations. Locality text can miss recordings; results are a bounded sample. Dates on recording cards are recording dates, not upload or collection dates. Source quality is xeno-canto’s A–E rating, with A highest.

Privacy & connection

Filters and imports run in this browser. Playing audio contacts xeno-canto; opening a source or licence link contacts that provider. The page never asks for an API key. A CoolCode counter records aggregate page opens without your filters or files.
